# Notion Cards Settings

This file stores your Notion credentials for the notion-cards plugin.

## How to get these values

### notion_token
1. Go to https://www.notion.so/my-integrations
2. Create or select an integration
3. Copy the "Internal Integration Secret"

### database_id
1. Open your Notion database
2. Copy the ID from the URL: `notion.so/{workspace}/{database_id}?v=...`

### owner_uuid
1. Go to Notion Settings → My Account
2. Your UUID is in the URL

### owner_name
Your display name (used in branch names, will be lowercased)
